Everything you need to know before you walk in on Sunday.

What to expect

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Most people dress smart-casual, but there's no dress code — we care more about you being here than what you're wearing.

Is there a children's ministry?

Yes. Children are welcomed into age-appropriate classes during the service so they can learn and worship at their own level.

What time should I arrive?

Aim to arrive 10–15 minutes early so you have time to find parking, get settled, and be welcomed before the service begins.

Where can I park?

On-site parking is available, with an usher on hand to direct you when you arrive.

Will someone welcome me?

Yes — our welcome team looks out for first-time faces at every service and will help you find your way around.

What happens during the service?

Worship, prayer, a message from the Word, and time to connect with others afterward. Services run for about two hours.

Still have questions?

Message us on WhatsApp any time — we're happy to answer anything before you make the trip.
